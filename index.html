What We Check

QR Shield runs 15+ security signals on every URL

🔒

HTTPS Enforcement

Plain HTTP means no encryption. We flag unencrypted URLs that expose your credentials to network attackers.

🎭

Brand Impersonation

Detects lookalike domains (paypa1.com, app1e.com) and brand names embedded in malicious hostnames.

✂️

URL Shorteners

Shortened links hide the real destination. We detect bit.ly, tinyurl and 10+ other shorteners.

🏗️

Suspicious Structure

Excessive subdomains, raw IP addresses, unusual ports, punycode homographs, and double extensions.

🔑

Keyword Detection

Credential-harvesting pages often contain keywords like "login", "verify", "secure", "reset", "wallet".

🤖

Gemini AI Model

Google Gemini AI analyzes the full URL context, social engineering cues, and phishing intent patterns.
